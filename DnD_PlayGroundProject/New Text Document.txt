########################### Quest flow and placement in the world ###########################
I think it would be better for the immersion of the game and easier to program if the player will only be able to do a single quest at a time.

Each quest will have some story in the world, DnD style. It will have a story, climax, reward and so on.

I want to avoid random grind quests such as kill 20 wolves.

While the player is not doing a quest, they will explore the world, be able to talk to people, kill things and so on, but once they accept a quest, they commited to either completeing it or abandoning it, without doing other quests.

They will stil be able to exlore, they just wont be able to take other quests.

This will help feel like the world more DnD like. I want it to feel like a DM is telling a story and building a campaign for the player.


Coding wise, this is also easier, Once the player accepts a quest, I can run a code to Set up the world for the quest. Place certain monsters somewhere, place unique npcs, populate certain zones and so on.

An important point is that the world is still a free open world, the player can choose to go where ever they want, its just that every time they accept a quest, they essentially start a mini campaign
