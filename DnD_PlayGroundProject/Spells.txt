Name: Fire Bolt
Damage: 1d10 fire
Range: 24 squares
Description: A bolt of fire streaks toward a creature. On a hit, it deals fire damage. Can target creatures at long range.

Name: Ray of Frost
Damage: 1d8 cold
Range: 12 squares
Description: A frigid beam of blue-white light slows a creature and deals cold damage.

Name: Eldritch Blast
Damage: 1d10 force
Range: 24 squares
Description: A beam of magical force streaks toward a creature. Can be scaled with levels. Cantrip for warlocks.

Name: Toll the Dead
Damage: 1d8 necrotic (or 1d12 if target is missing HP)
Range: 12 squares
Description: Rings a mystical bell that damages the target with necrotic energy.

Name: Chill Touch
Damage: 1d8 necrotic
Range: 24 squares
Description: A ghostly skeletal hand appears to touch the target, dealing necrotic damage and preventing healing.

Name: Shocking Grasp
Damage: 1d8 lightning
Range: 1 square (melee touch)
Description: You deliver a jolt of electricity. Target cannot take reactions until next turn.

Name: Sacred Flame
Damage: 1d8 radiant
Range: 12 squares
Description: Flames descend on the target, dealing radiant damage. Target can make a Dexterity saving throw for half damage.

Name: Sword Burst
Damage: 1d6 force
Range: Self (1 square radius)
Description: Creates a burst of spectral swords around you, damaging all creatures within 1 square.

Name: Green-Flame Blade
Damage: 1d8 + secondary fire damage
Range: Melee weapon attack (1 square)
Description: You make a melee attack; on hit, a secondary creature takes fire damage.

Name: Booming Blade
Damage: 1d8 + secondary thunder damage
Range: Melee weapon attack (1 square)
Description: Melee attack; if the target moves, it takes thunder damage on its turn.
