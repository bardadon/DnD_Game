RAGE PROGRESSION

Level 1–2: 2 uses (+2 damage)
Level 3–5: 3 uses (+2 damage)
Level 6–8: 4 uses (+2 damage)
Level 9–11: 4 uses (+3 damage)
Level 12–16: 5 uses (+3 damage)
Level 17–19: 6 uses (+4 damage)
Level 20: Unlimited uses (+4 damage)

NOTES ON BERSERKER FEATURES (plain explanation)

Frenzy (Level 3):

Bonus Action melee attack every turn while Frenzied.

Gain 1 level of Exhaustion when Rage ends.

Mindless Rage (Level 6):

Immune to charm and fright while raging.

If already affected, effects pause during Rage.

Intimidating Presence (Level 10):

Action: frighten a creature within 30 ft.

Target repeats CHA save at end of each turn to end effect.

Ends early if you move too far away or do nothing aggressive.

Retaliation (Level 14):

When a creature hits you in melee, you may use your Reaction to make a melee attack back.
